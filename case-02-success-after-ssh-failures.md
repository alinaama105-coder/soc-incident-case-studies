Case 02 — Successful SSH Login After Failures

Scenario

A successful SSH authentication occurs after repeated failed attempts.

Initial Triage

- Identify the successful login.
- Review preceding failures.
- Compare source IPs.
- Identify the username and target host.
- Review the time between the failures and success.

Investigation

Search for additional activity from the same source and review what occurred after the successful authentication.

Decision

A successful login following repeated failures requires context. If the activity cannot be explained as expected user behaviour, escalate for further investigation.
