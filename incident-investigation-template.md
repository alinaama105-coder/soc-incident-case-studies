Incident Investigation Template

Alert

Record the alert name, source and severity.

Initial Triage

- What triggered the alert?
- Which system is affected?
- Which user is involved?
- What is the source IP?
- When did the activity occur?

Evidence

Record the relevant logs, timestamps, users, hosts, IP addresses and other indicators.

Investigation

Document the searches performed and what the evidence shows.

Finding

State what can be concluded from the available evidence without assuming facts that have not been verified.

Action

Record any escalation, containment or other authorised action.

Closure

Document the final outcome and reason for closure.
